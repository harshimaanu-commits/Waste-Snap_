ABOUT WASTESNAP

Small reports. Real change.

WasteSnap is a community-focused platform designed to make reporting local waste problems simple and accessible.

Instead of ignoring overflowing bins, dumped waste or dirty public spaces, residents can report the problem and follow its progress.

By connecting citizens, workers and administrators, WasteSnap helps turn individual reports into cleaner neighbourhoods.
